        <h2 class="reveal">VP Design searches need more than portfolios</h2>
        <p class="reveal" style="margin-top:16px;font-size:17px">A portfolio shows craft. It doesn't show whether a leader can standardize patterns across products, clarify decision rights with Product and Engineering, or make AI adoption real instead of theater.</p>
        <div class="pains">
          <div class="pain reveal"><h3>Portfolio bias</h3><p>Craft on a slide says nothing about governance across a multi-product platform.</p></div>
          <div class="pain reveal"><h3>Decision ambiguity</h3><p>When Product and UX both own everything, nobody owns anything. Rework is the tax.</p></div>
          <div class="pain reveal"><h3>System drift</h3><p>A design system without governance and adoption metrics becomes a component junk drawer.</p></div>
          <div class="pain reveal"><h3>AI theater</h3><p>Pilots that demo well and change nothing about how teams actually ship.</p></div>
