The care plan

The first 90 days

01

Listen first

Sit with the design teams, Escalations, Outbound Product, and Customer Success. The highest-impact user pain points are already known inside the building; the job is to rank them and put names on them.

02

Clarify decision rights

Name what UX owns, what Product owns, and what they decide together, in writing. Ambiguity is the tax on every release, and reducing it is a deliverable, not a vibe.

03

Govern the system

Contribution model, adoption metrics, and interaction standards for the design system across products and teams, so consistency stops depending on heroics.

04

Make AI adoption real

Governed AI workflows for design, content, and research, measured on quality and delivery speed. The same operating model that already works at a 45-person scale, tuned to healthcare's stakes.

Each with a success metric agreed with Product and Engineering leadership, on paper, in the first month.
